# N1 — Does an expert striatal urgency ramp predict *when* the mouse responds, beyond movement? An HONEST CONTROLLED NEGATIVE

**Date:** 2026-06-30 · **Subject:** BG_046 (n=1) · **Status:** done (controlled negative)
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-06-29-N1-neural-urgency-ramp-timing-latent-design.md`
**Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-06-29-N1-neural-urgency-ramp-timing-latent-plan.md`
**Library:** `src/visdetect/analysis/neural_latents.py` · **Scripts:** `scripts/neural_latents/{n1_c1_gate,n1_c1b_within_fa,n1_c1c_ramp_slope,_synthetic_recovery,run_n1}.py`
**Cache:** `data/cache/neural_latents/{n1_c1_gate,n1_c1b_within_fa,n1_c1c_ramp_slope,n1_synthetic_verdict,n1_results}.json` · **Figure:** `FIGURES/neural_latents/BG_046/fig_n1_summary.png`

**The question.** In expert BG_046 medial striatum, does **pre-decision (waiting-window) population activity carry an urgency signal that predicts *when* the animal will respond (`decision_time`), over and above generic motor preparation?** Neural arm of the post-TF-null decision-spine (A1/E1 commitment-ramp + the B8 timing facet); the place the state-label circularity breaks, since a neural correlate is labeler-independent. Pre-registered GATE: (1) pre-decision activity predicts response timing above a trial-shuffle null; (2) it survives projecting out a fresh lick/motor coding direction ("beyond motor prep"); NOTE B required prediction **graded within trial type** (esp. within hits), not hit-vs-FA classification.

> **Glossary.** *Urgency/timing ramp* — a build-up of "now is about when the change should come" in population activity. *Self-timed (FA) lick* — an anticipatory false-alarm lick on the animal's own clock, before any real change. *Hit lick* — a lick after a real, externally-set change. *Lick leakage* — the decoder reading lick-timing off activity *during/just after* the lick, not the pre-lick waiting period. *Movement-matching* — holding movement-amount ~constant so a timing prediction cannot just be a movement-amount prediction.

---

## 1. Result — a tight, fully-controlled NULL

**Verdict: controlled negative.** After leakage-filtering and movement-matching, **neither** the mean activity level **nor** the per-unit ramp slope predicts self-timed (FA) lick timing: both collapse to **r ≈ 0.02** with bootstrap-over-sessions 95% CIs that span zero (session = unit of replication; 28/29 expert anchors; `11092025` fail-safe-skipped on a Baseline_ON length guard). Decoding is **within session** (units are within-session QC, not cross-session tracked), aggregated over sessions; a regression test guards against the cross-session/global-Spearman Simpson trap.

**Within-trial-type readout (NOTE B) — the pooled decode is hit-vs-FA structure, and within-HIT is a clean negative control:**

| window | within-HIT r | within-FA r (raw) |
|---|---:|---:|
| early [0.5,2.5] s | **0.061** | 0.563 |
| mid [2.0,4.0] s | **0.046** | 0.515 |
| late [4.0,6.0] s | **0.054** | 0.337 |

**within-HIT ≈ 0.05 is a clean, leakage-free NEGATIVE CONTROL** (hits lick > 6 s, *after* every read-out window → the exogenous, stimulus-set hit time *cannot* be predicted from pre-change activity, and correctly is not). It certifies the pipeline is not leaking and not inflated by trial-type structure. The only genuinely self-timed signal is within-FA.

## 2. Sensitivity / power — the null is a TRUE NEGATIVE, not a power failure

The pipeline demonstrably **detects timing signal when it is present**, so the post-control null is a real null:

- **(a) Synthetic recovery (`n1_synthetic_recovery.py`, Task 5).** On simulated cohorts the decoder **recovers planted response-timing** (`recovers = True`: φ-urgency r = 0.54 and pure-motor r = 0.81 both beat null), and the movement control behaves correctly — the per-session motor-CD projection **kills** a pure-motor signal (→ 0.01) while **sparing** an orthogonal urgency signal (0.54) (`motor_killed = True`). The control battery works.
- **(b) The raw pre-filter signal is large and detected** — within-FA raw r = **0.56** (mean) / **0.33** (slope). The method finds a strong correlation; it is the *leakage filter + matching* that removes it, not a lack of statistical power.

So when a genuine, leakage-free, movement-independent timing signal exists (synthetic), the pipeline finds it; on the real data, after the same controls, it is absent.

## 3. METHODS CAUTIONARY TALE — the reusable headline

> **Within-trial-type timing decodes are massively inflated by lick leakage: r 0.56 → 0.03 (mean) and 0.33 → 0.02 (slope)** once FA licks are required to fall **≥ 0.25 s after** the read-out window.

When you decode *when* an animal will act from neural activity in a window, and a nontrivial fraction of actions land inside that window (here **15–28 %** of FA licks: early 26.8 %, mid 15.0 %, late 28.0 %), the decoder reads the action off peri/post-action activity and reports a large, spurious correlation. The three-part hygiene that fixes it:

1. **Leakage filter FIRST** — keep only trials whose action is comfortably *after* the window.
2. **Movement-matching** — hold movement-amount (motor-CD magnitude) ~constant; rank **partial-Spearman** is the conservative primary, within-strata the lenient companion, multi-dim motor-subspace projection the secondary.
3. **A within-condition leakage-free negative control** — here within-HIT (exogenous, stimulus-set action time, unpredictable from pre-action activity); its ≈ 0.05 certifies the pipeline is clean.

Skipping any one would have reported a false "striatal urgency ramp predicts response timing." This transfers to any self-timed / free-response decoding (lick, reach, saccade) over a pre-action window.

## 4. Behavioural anchor — temporal expectation IS real (B8 figure F4)

Temporal expectation is independently established **behaviourally** by B8's **figure F4** (`FIGURES/decision_latents/BG_046/fig_b8_F4_timing.png`): a true, **survival-corrected anticipatory FA-hazard that rises toward the expected change time** — the mouse's self-timed licking accelerates as the expected change approaches. So the precise claim is:

> **Temporal expectation is behaviourally real, but has no *separable striatal urgency-ramp correlate of self-timed timing* in this population / read-out** — NOT "there is no temporal expectation."

F4 also makes the neural null structurally expected, and explains why the φ-shaped bump is invisible in the clean pre-movement window (its distinguishing peak sits at/after the change time, entangled with detection + movement).

## 5. Scope & limitations (honest)

- **Single subject (n = 1, BG_046).** Architected for a later hierarchical F3 cohort; no cross-subject claim.
- **Units = `good_and_stable_ids`** (within-session QC), **cell types POOLED** (no cell-type resolution in the decode). Per-session yields (reported): **good_and_stable units/session median 200, range 46–292**; clean (post-window) FA trials/session **early median 104 [30–290], mid 85 [23–227], late 40 [11–88]**; 28/29 sessions contributed.
- **D1 vs D2 is not waveform-separable** (optotag yield = 3 confirmed units), so the **cell-type-resolved spine question (D1/D2 push-pull) is not testable here** and cell-typing (SPN/FSI) was not run — there is no robust ramp to decompose regardless.
- **No external movement channel** (BG_046 has no usable video/facemap/pupil; the `video_sync` caches are sync-fitting artifacts), so "beyond motor prep" is asked against a neural-internal motor axis, and there is **no movement-free window** (motor axis significant in 68–93 % of sessions).
- **Short self-timed lead / few clean FA trials** (esp. late window) bound the achievable within-FA power.
- **Conceptual caveat (important):** for *self-timed* actions, **urgency and motor commitment may be one and the same** (basal-ganglia action-gating: the ramp that times the decision *is* the ramp that commits the movement). If so, "no urgency *beyond* motor" is a **true feature of the system, not an analysis absence** — there may be nothing to separate.
- **φ-specificity** (φ-shaped vs generic ramp) is **underpowered by construction** over the achievable pre-μ window (φ-weight vs ramp-weight profiles ≈ 0.89 collinear; synthetic ΔCV ≈ +0.013, CI spans 0 even with signal planted) — a test-limitation, not evidence of absence; not run.

---

### Bottom line

The pipeline detects timing signal when it exists (synthetic recovery; raw r 0.56/0.33). On real expert striatum, after a leakage filter and movement-matching, **there is no separable urgency-ramp correlate of self-timed (FA) lick timing — in either the activity level or the ramp slope** (r ≈ 0.02, CIs span 0), while **within-HIT ≈ 0** is a clean exogenous negative control. Temporal expectation is **behaviourally real (B8 F4)** but lacks a separable striatal self-timed-urgency correlate in this n=1 population/read-out — possibly because, for self-timed actions, urgency and motor commitment are unified. The most transferable result is the **methods cautionary tale**: within-trial-type timing decodes inflate ~0.5 → ~0.02 via lick leakage, and the fix is leakage-filter + movement-matching + a within-condition leakage-free negative control.
