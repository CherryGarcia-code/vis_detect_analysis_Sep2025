> **Plain-language glossary.** *Urgency / timing ramp* = a build-up of "now is about
> the time the change should come" in population activity, toward the expected change
> time. *Self-timed (FA) lick* = an anticipatory false-alarm lick the animal emits on
> its own clock, before any real stimulus change. *Hit lick* = a lick after a real,
> externally-set change. *Lick leakage* = the decoder cheating by reading lick-timing
> off activity that is *during or just after* the lick itself, rather than the pre-lick
> waiting period. *Movement-matching* = holding "how much the animal is moving"
> ~constant so a timing prediction cannot just be a movement-amount prediction.

---

## 1. The question

In expert BG_046 medial striatum, does **pre-decision (waiting-window) population
activity carry an urgency signal that predicts *when* the animal will respond
(`decision_time`), over and above generic motor preparation?** This is the neural arm
of the post-TF-null decision-spine (A1/E1 commitment-ramp + the B8 timing facet); it
is where the state-label circularity breaks, because a neural correlate is
labeler-independent.

The pre-registered existence GATE had two prongs: (1) pre-decision activity predicts
response timing above a trial-shuffle null; (2) the prediction survives projecting out
a freshly-built, validated lick/motor coding direction (i.e. it is "beyond motor
prep"). NOTE B required the prediction to be **graded within trial type** (especially
within hits), not merely hit-vs-FA classification.

## 2. Behaviour anchor — temporal expectation is REAL (B8 figure F4)

Before the neural test, temporal expectation is already established **behaviourally**
by B8's **figure F4** (`FIGURES/decision_latents/BG_046/fig_b8_F4_timing.png`): a
true, **survival-corrected anticipatory FA-hazard that rises toward the expected
change time**. The mouse's own self-timed (false-alarm) licking accelerates as the
expected change approaches — that is temporal expectation, measured in behaviour.

**Consequence for N1:** N1 does *not* need to prove temporal expectation neurally; F4
already did, behaviourally. A neural null is therefore **structurally expected, not a
weakness** — and F4 also explains *why* the φ-specific bump is invisible in the clean
pre-movement window (see §4 and §5).

## 3. Neural result — a fully-controlled NEGATIVE

**Verdict: controlled negative.** There is no leakage-free, movement-matched striatal
correlate of self-timed (FA) lick timing in pre-change population activity — neither
the mean activity level nor the per-unit ramp slope.

Sessions: **28 of 29** fitted-expert anchors contributed; `11092025` was fail-safe
skipped (`Baseline_ON` had 537 entries vs 538 trials — a join-integrity guard, not a
silent drop). Units per session were `good_and_stable_ids` (within-session QC),
decoded **within each session** with bootstrap CIs **over sessions** (session = unit
of replication; cross-session columns are different neurons, so no cross-session
pooling and no pooled global Spearman — the Simpson-paradox trap is guarded by a
regression test).

### 3a. The within-HIT clean negative control (this validates the decode)

The pooled response-time decode beat null in all windows (mean r ≈ 0.44–0.45), **but
that is largely hit-vs-FA trial-type separation, not graded urgency.** Reading
*within* trial type (NOTE B):

| window | within-HIT r | within-FA r (raw) |
|--------|-------------:|------------------:|
| early [0.5, 2.5] s | **0.061** | 0.563 |
| mid   [2.0, 4.0] s | **0.046** | 0.515 |
| late  [4.0, 6.0] s | **0.054** | 0.337 |

**Within-HIT ≈ 0.05 is a clean, leakage-free NEGATIVE CONTROL, not a failure.** Hits
lick > 6 s, *after* every read-out window, so the externally-set (exogenous) hit time
*cannot* be predicted from pre-change activity — and correctly is not. A decoder that
read out true urgency would still fail here because the hit time is set by the
stimulus, not the animal. So the near-zero within-hit value confirms the pipeline is
not leaking and is not inflated by trial-type structure. The only genuinely
*self-timed* signal is the within-FA timing.

### 3b. The self-timed (FA) decode is overwhelmingly LICK LEAKAGE

The raw within-FA decode looks strong, but **15–28 % of FA licks fall *inside* the
read-out window** (early 26.8 %, mid 15.0 %, late 28.0 %), so the decoder reads
`decision_time` off peri/post-lick activity. The fix, in mandatory order: **leakage
filter FIRST** (keep only `decision_time ≥ window-end + 0.25 s`), **then
movement-match** (partial-Spearman primary; within-strata is the lenient companion;
multi-dim motor-subspace projection secondary).

**MEAN read-out, early window (leads: best raw signal, least movement, most clean
trials; early activity predicting a lick *seconds later* is itself evidence against
pure peri-movement motor prep):**

| stage | within-FA r | 95% CI | n |
|-------|-----------:|--------|---|
| raw (all FA) | 0.559 | — | 28 sessions |
| leakage-filtered | **0.027** | [−0.027, 0.080] | ~104 clean FA/session |
| matched — within-strata (lenient) | 0.024 | [−0.021, 0.067] | — |
| matched — partial-Spearman (**primary**) | **0.010** | [−0.041, 0.061] | — |
| subspace-projected (secondary) | 0.022 | [−0.035, 0.076] | — |

Mid and late windows are the same or worse (mid matched-partial 0.002, late −0.073).

### 3c. The one pre-specified ramp-appropriate read-out (SLOPE) — also null

A null on the *mean* is not a null on the *slope*; a ramp-to-threshold's
decision-relevant content is the **rate of rise**. So we ran exactly **one**
additional, pre-specified, ramp-appropriate read-out — the per-unit OLS **slope** of z
vs time across the window — through the identical leakage-filtered + movement-matched
cascade. **It is also a controlled NULL:**

| stage | within-FA SLOPE r (early) | 95% CI |
|-------|-------------------------:|--------|
| raw (all FA) | 0.332 | — |
| leakage-filtered | **0.023** | [−0.015, 0.063] |
| matched — within-strata (lenient) | 0.011 | [−0.027, 0.053] |
| matched — partial-Spearman (**primary**) | **0.019** | [−0.020, 0.058] |
| subspace-projected (secondary) | −0.021 | [−0.057, 0.016] |

Both readouts collapse from ~0.5 / ~0.33 to ~0.02 once leakage is removed and movement
is matched, with bootstrap-over-sessions CIs that span zero. **This was the LAST
read-out by design** — adding more after a null would be fishing — so N1 finalizes as
a controlled negative.

> A note on the 6b JSON `verdict` string. The within-FA MEAN cascade JSON
> (`n1_c1b_within_fa.json`) carries an optimistic per-script `verdict` of "PASSES" for
> the early window, because its decision rule (matched-partial/strata beats the
> matched shuffle null + 2 SD) is satisfied only because the matched null itself sits
> at ≈ −0.043 (so even r ≈ 0.024 nominally clears it). The **bootstrap CI over
> sessions for the matched-partial estimate spans zero** ([−0.041, 0.061]), the effect
> size is ~0.01–0.02, and the ramp-slope readout (6c) is unambiguously null. The
> honest, conservative read across **both** readouts is therefore a **controlled
> negative**; this write-up supersedes the 6b script's per-window pass/fail string.

### 3d. Method validation (synthetic, before any real claim)

`n1_synthetic_verdict.json`: the decoder **recovers** planted response-timing in both
a φ-urgency and a pure-motor population (`recovers = True`); the motor-CD projection
**kills** the pure-motor case while sparing an orthogonal φ-urgency case
(`motor_killed = True`). φ-vs-ramp separability on the achievable pre-μ window is
`False` — underpowered by construction (see §5). The control battery works; the real
null is a real null.

## 4. METHODS CAUTIONARY TALE — the reusable headline

The most transferable result here is a methods lesson:

> **Within-trial-type timing decodes inflate from ~0.5 to ~0.02 via lick leakage.**

When you decode *when* an animal will act from neural activity in a window, and a
nontrivial fraction of actions land inside that window, the decoder reads the action
off peri/post-action activity and reports a large, spurious correlation. The fix is a
three-part hygiene:

1. **Leakage filter FIRST** — keep only trials whose action is comfortably *after* the
   read-out window (here `decision_time ≥ window-end + 0.25 s`).
2. **Movement-matching** — hold the movement-amount (motor coding-direction magnitude)
   ~constant; partial-Spearman on ranks is the conservative primary, within-strata the
   lenient companion, multi-dim motor-subspace projection the secondary.
3. **A within-condition leakage-free negative control** — here within-HIT, where the
   action time is exogenous (stimulus-set) and *cannot* be predicted from pre-action
   activity; its ~0.05 value certifies the pipeline is clean.

Skipping any one of these would have reported a "striatal urgency ramp predicts
response timing" finding that is an artifact. This generalizes to any self-timed /
free-response decoding (lick, reach, saccade) over a pre-action window.

## 5. What would be needed to detect a genuine pre-movement urgency signal

The negative is informative but bounded. To give a real pre-movement urgency signal a
fair chance one would need:

- **A cleaner, continuous movement read-out** (facemap / pupil / DLC / motion-onset).
  BG_046 has **no usable video/movement channel** (the `video_sync` caches are
  sync-fitting artifacts only), so all movement control here is neural-internal (a
  fresh lick/motor coding direction). A genuine movement regressor would let urgency be
  partialled from movement directly rather than via an internal proxy, and there is
  **no movement-free window** (the motor axis is significant in 68–93 % of sessions),
  which limits how cleanly "beyond motor prep" can be asked.
- **A task with a longer self-timed lead.** The clean (post-window) FA trials are few
  per session (median ~104 early, ~40 late) and the anticipatory lead is short; a
  paradigm with a longer, more variable self-timed interval (or an explicit timed-hold)
  would put more of the ramp in an unambiguously pre-movement window.
- **More subjects** (F3 cohort: BG_031/038/039/049) to move from n=1 to a hierarchical
  estimate, and possibly cell-type-resolved population sizes large enough to ask the
  question per SPN/FSI sub-population.

## 6. C3 (across-session u-graded) and φ-specificity status

- **C3 — across-session u-graded test (neural urgency-ramp amplitude vs the B8 fitted
  timing-amplitude *u*, mood-controlled): NOT computed (document-don't-fish).** C3 is
  **premised on a robust neural ramp amplitude, which N1 did not find.** Correlating a
  non-surviving ramp amplitude against *u* would be spurious — there is no validated
  per-session ramp amplitude to grade. It is therefore stated plainly here and **not
  run**; it is not interpretable as support for the timing latent.
- **φ-specificity (does a φ-shaped temporal-expectation basis beat a generic monotonic
  ramp): N/A — not run.** There is no robust ramp whose specificity could be tested,
  and the test is **underpowered by construction** regardless: over the achievable
  pre-μ read-out window the φ-weight and ramp-weight *profiles* are ≈ 0.89 collinear
  (synthetic ΔCV ≈ +0.013, CI spans 0 even with a real signal planted). This is a
  test-limitation ("the within-window φ-weighting cannot separate the anticipation
  shape from a generic ramp over the achievable window"), **not** "we tested for
  temporal-expectation coding and found none." F4 already establishes temporal
  expectation behaviourally; over the pre-change window the anticipatory signal is a
  rising ramp and φ's distinguishing peak sits at/after the change time (entangled with
  detection + movement), outside the clean pre-movement window.
- **Cell-typing (C2, SPN/FSI breakdown): SKIPPED.** With no robust timing ramp to
  decompose, there is nothing to break down by broad-SPN / narrow-FSI; the gate's
  cell-type leg does not run.

## 7. Limitations

- **n = 1 (BG_046).** Single subject; architected for a later hierarchical F3 cohort,
  but no cross-subject claim is made.
- **Single population read-out per window.** Within-session population decode of mean z
  (and per-unit slope); not every conceivable urgency estimator was tried — but the
  two run (mean, slope) are the pre-specified, decision-relevant ones, and the
  discipline was to stop after the one ramp-appropriate readout rather than fish.
- **No external movement channel** (see §5); "beyond motor prep" is asked against a
  neural-internal motor axis and there is no movement-free window.
- **Short self-timed lead / few clean FA trials** per session bound the achievable
  power for the within-FA decode (and for any pre-movement window).
- **D1 vs D2 deferred** (not waveform-separable; optotag yield = 3), independent of
  this null.

---

### Bottom line

F4 (behaviour) → temporal expectation **exists**. N1 (neural) → there is **no
leakage-free, movement-matched striatal correlate of self-timed (FA) lick timing** in
pre-change activity, for either the mean level or the ramp slope — an **honest
controlled negative**, with a reusable **methods cautionary tale** (lick leakage
inflates within-type timing decodes ~0.5 → ~0.02; the fix is the leakage filter +
movement-matching + the within-hit negative control).
